3. Grading Scale & Computation Rules

All grade point averages are computed strictly using standard university credit-weighted formulas:

  • SGPA equals the sum of course credits multiplied by grade points, divided by total semester registered credits.
  • CGPA equals the sum of all completed course credits multiplied by grade points, divided by cumulative registered credits.
  • Passing grade threshold is 4.0 points (Grade P). Any grade of F awards 0 points and counts as an active course backlog.
