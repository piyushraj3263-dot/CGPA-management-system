Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect

The Student CGPA Management System processes institutional academic data strictly necessary for record maintenance and GPA computation. This includes:

  • Student identification attributes: Full legal name, institutional identification number (UID), and academic department/branch.
  • Academic performance data: Course identifiers, registered credit values, semester designations, and awarded grade points.
  • System logs: Timestamps of academic record creation and grade modifications.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Collected information is utilized exclusively for:

  • Calculating Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) according to university formulas.
  • Generating verified student academic transcripts and performance cards.
  • Maintaining referential data integrity across institutional database systems.

3. Data Retention and Security

All academic records are stored in normalized relational databases with primary key, foreign key, and unique constraints. Access is restricted to authorized academic personnel. We do not sell, license, or transmit student academic data to external third parties.

4. Student Rights and Record Correction

Enrolled students hold the right to inspect their recorded grades and submit re-evaluation or correction requests through official departmental administration.
